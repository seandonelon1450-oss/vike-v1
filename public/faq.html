Plain answers

Questions.

Does Arbor use AI?

Yes — plainly. V.I.K.e uses AI to read message patterns and draft replies you can review. Right now she runs in demo mode, which is pattern matching, not AI — and the app says so on screen. When real AI analysis is switched on, the status badge changes to say "AI analysis," and the provider is named.

What's not AI: your evidence file (a simple log in your browser), your invite codes, and the tallies we keep (plain counts, no learning). We never train any model on your data — not ours, not anyone's.

What does V.I.K.e actually do?

You paste messages from a match — or anyone. She reads them for patterns: love-bombing, future-faking, off-app pressure, story inconsistencies, control moves. Then she gives you a plain five-line read and drafts replies you can copy if you choose to. She never sends anything herself.

Is V.I.K.e a person?

No. Vicky is clearly-disclosed software — a consistent voice for a screening tool, not a human. There is a real human behind the product: write to seandonelon.apps@gmail.com and a person answers.

Is this a crisis service?

No. V.I.K.e is a screening tool — she cannot intervene, and she never contacts anyone. If you or someone you love is in danger: call 911. For free, confidential help any time: the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 1-800-799-7233 (or text START to 88788), or the Louisiana statewide hotline at 1-888-411-1333.

Where does my evidence file live?

Only in this browser, on this device. Our server never sees it, can't back it up, and can't recover it. Export a copy from the app or the Your data page; delete it any time in one tap.

Do you sell my data or train AI on it?

Neither. Never sold — not to advertisers, data brokers, providers, or partners. Never used to train models — not ours, not anyone's. The full 10-point promise is on the Your data page, word for word.

Why does the button say "Request an invite" instead of letting me pay?

The founding circle — 100 seats at $39/month, locked for life — opens after our payment system passes its full test (webhook wired, one real test charge, one refund). Until then, we don't take cards. Leave your email and we'll write when your seat is ready.

What does V.I.K.e stand for?

That's on the About page — we keep the full name there, and keep the headlines about the outcome: get home safe.
