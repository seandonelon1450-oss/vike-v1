Trust, plainly

  • What you paste is analyzed on our server. V1 trades on-device processing for speed while we build the private, on-device V2. That is the honest tradeoff.
  • We do not train on your data. Inputs exist only for the request, then they’re gone. The server keeps no logs of what you pasted and no database at all.
  • Your evidence file never leaves this device. It lives in this browser’s local storage. We cannot see it, back it up, or recover it.
  • Delete means delete. Removing entries clears them from this browser. There is nothing on any server to clear, because nothing was ever sent there.
  • V.I.K.e is clearly-disclosed AI (or, in demo mode, clearly-labeled pattern matching). She analyzes words and patterns. She does not detect lies, read minds, contact anyone, or intervene.

V2 north star: fully on-device processing — nothing you paste ever leaves your phone. We will say so only when it is true.
