How Vicky reads people — the 5 personality lines

She doesn't read minds. She reads patterns — the same five lines, every time:

  1. Texting rhythm — how he handles closeness and distance: pursuit, withdrawal, testing, popping back up.
  2. Who the conversation is really about — does he track your feelings, or only his own?
  3. Control moves — isolation, monitoring, decisions that stop being yours.
  4. Hot-and-cold rewards — love-bombing into withdrawal; affection on a random schedule.
  5. The consistency ledger — story vs. story across time. Week one vs. week four.

Every result shows each line's name, the markers she actually saw (with his own words), what it tends to mean, and one concrete thing to do about it. She describes what she can see in the words — never a diagnosis, never a label. She's a screening tool, not a doctor's office. Demo mode runs on pattern matching, not AI.

    Trust, plainly

    • What you paste is analyzed on our server. V1 trades on-device processing for speed while we build the private, on-device V2. That is the honest tradeoff.
    • We do not train on your data. Inputs exist only for the request, then they're gone. The server keeps no logs of what you pasted and no database at all.
    • Your evidence file never leaves this device. It lives in this browser's local storage. We cannot see it, back it up, or recover it.
    • Delete means delete. Removing entries clears them from this browser. There is nothing on any server to clear, because nothing was ever sent there.
    • Screenshots: there are none. V.I.K.e doesn't accept photo or screenshot uploads — you paste text — so there's nothing uploaded, nothing stored, and no photo metadata to strip.
    • V.I.K.e is clearly-disclosed AI (or, in demo mode, clearly-labeled pattern matching). She analyzes words and patterns. She does not detect lies, read minds, contact anyone, or intervene.

    V2 north star: fully on-device processing — nothing you paste ever leaves your phone. We will say so only when it is true.
