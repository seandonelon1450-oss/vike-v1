The charter — word for word

  1. Your data is yours. We hold it for you, to do work for you. Take it with you any time.
  2. Never sold. Not to advertisers, data brokers, providers, or partners. No ad trackers.
  3. Never used to train models. Not ours, not anyone's. The list of providers is public.
  4. Export in one tap. Everything, in a readable format (PDF + JSON/CSV).
  5. Delete in one tap. Gone from the live system right away, and from backups within 30 days.
  6. Encrypted. In transit and at rest. Device-only rooms we can't read at all.
  7. Collect the minimum. Each feature asks only for what it needs. Every connector is opt-in.
  8. Your choice of home. Our encrypted cloud by default, or on your device only.
  9. People see only what you share. Team access is limited, logged, and visible to you.
  10. No quiet changes. New use of your data? We ask first, in plain words. Silence never counts as yes.

On the export promise: text and JSON work today; PDF is coming soon, and we'll say so here when it lands.

What we hold — the whole picture

  • What you paste for screening. Travels to V.I.K.e's server so it can be analyzed — then it's gone. The server keeps no copy and no log of what you wrote.
  • Your evidence file. Lives only in this browser, on this device. The server never sees it. Clearing your browser data or losing your phone means losing the file — export it if it matters to you.
  • Screenshots: there are none. V.I.K.e doesn't accept photo or screenshot uploads — you paste text — so there's nothing uploaded, nothing stored, and no photo metadata to strip.
  • Nominations and invite requests. Names and contact details you type into "Nominate a woman" or "Request an invite" are saved on our server (which resets if the server restarts). To export or delete these, write to seandonelon.apps@gmail.com — a real person handles it.
  • Anonymous tallies. Which pages get visited, how often features get used. No cookies, no name, no email, no account. Reset if the server restarts.
  • Payments (when checkout opens). Card details go to Stripe — they never touch V.I.K.e's servers, and we never see or store them.

Providers

Today V.I.K.e runs in demo mode: analysis happens with on-server pattern matching, and nothing leaves V.I.K.e's server at all. When real AI analysis is switched on, pasted text is also sent to the configured AI service under that service's data policy — and the service name is shown in the app's status badge. We never train any model on your data, ever.

What we will never do

  • Sell your data. There is nothing to sell — we barely hold anything.
  • Read your evidence file. We technically can't.
  • Contact anyone on your behalf, or share anything about you with anyone, for any reason.
