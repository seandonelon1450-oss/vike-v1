About V.I.K.e

Get home safe.

V.I.K.e is a private safety read for the dating world. You show her the messages; she reads the patterns — the pressure, the inconsistencies, the moves — and drafts what you could say back. She never sends anything. You decide, always.

What she does

  • Reads the patterns. Five lines, every time: texting rhythm, who the conversation is really about, control moves, hot-and-cold rewards, the consistency ledger.
  • Drafts your replies. Every draft is labeled "Drafted for you · review before sending." Copy one only if you choose to.
  • Keeps your file. Your own log of what happened, on your device only. Export it or delete it in one tap.
  • Plans the evening. A date plan and a "home safe?" check-in with a friend you trust.

What she never does

  • Never sends anything. No message, no email, no call leaves without your tap.
  • Never diagnoses. She describes what she can see in the words — never a label, never lie detection, never a medical opinion.
  • Never trains on you. Your data is never used to train models — not ours, not anyone's.
  • Never sells you. No ad trackers, no data brokers, no quiet changes.

The name

V.I.K.e — Vigilant Intuition, Kinetic Yoke, escort.

Vigilant intuition that moves with you; a yoke that steadies rather than steers; an escort's instinct to get you home safe.
